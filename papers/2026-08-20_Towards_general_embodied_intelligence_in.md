# Towards general embodied intelligence: integrating large language models, knowledge bases, and reasoning capabilities to build the next generation of AI agents

- **Category:** Robotics
- **Date:** 2026-08-20
- **Link:** http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19794v1

---
Here's a summary of the research paper in Markdown:

---

### Problem:

Large Language Models (LLMs) are powerful but fundamentally disembodied, lacking grounded understanding of the physical world, struggling with symbol grounding, prone to hallucinations, and having limited memory and world models. Existing Embodied AI (EAI) agents, while capable in physical environments, often rely on rigid, task-specific controls and exhibit limited autonomy in complex decision-making, semantic comprehension, and long-horizon reasoning. There's a significant gap in systematically integrating the cognitive strengths of LLMs, the structured knowledge of Knowledge Bases (KBs), and explicit Reasoning Ability (RA) with the sensorimotor capabilities of embodied agents to achieve General Embodied Intelligence (GEI). Current literature lacks a unified architecture, module coordination mechanisms, and a comprehensive framework for perception-cognition-action.

### Method:

This paper conducts a comprehensive survey and systematic review of the emerging interdisciplinary landscape at the intersection of LLMs, KBs, RA, and EAI. It reviews the evolution of LLM-centered intelligent systems, analyzes LLM architectures, pre-training methods, and inference mechanisms, and examines EAI paradigms. The core method involves:
1.  **Proposing a unified framework:** The paper builds a GEI technical architecture that illustrates the synergy among LLMs (as the core), KBs, RA, and embodiment, supporting perception, reasoning, and action capabilities.
2.  **Analyzing integration mechanisms:** It deeply explores how these modules can be fused to construct an intelligent system.
3.  **Identifying key challenges:** The paper identifies five critical research directions: efficient LLM deployment, closed-loop knowledge integration, hybrid symbolic-neural reasoning, perception-action grounding, and continual learning.
4.  **Providing a roadmap:** Through this systematic analysis, the paper offers a comprehensive technical roadmap for future development.

### Impact:

This survey provides a comprehensive roadmap and theoretical support for developing the next generation of adaptive, multimodal AI agents capable of operating in complex, dynamic settings. By systematizing the integration of LLMs, KBs, and reasoning with physical embodiment, it accelerates the trajectory towards General Embodied Intelligence (GEI). The proposed unified framework and the identified key challenges provide crucial guidance for researchers, fostering advancements that will enable AI agents to interpret complex commands, reason over multimodal observations, manage memory, utilize tools, and generate context-aware action plans in real-world scenarios.
