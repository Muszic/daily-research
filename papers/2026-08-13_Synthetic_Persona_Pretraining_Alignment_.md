## Synthetic Persona Pretraining: Alignment from Token Zero

### Problem

Current approaches to aligning large language models (LLMs) typically introduce the desired assistant identity and values *after* pretraining, during post-training (e.g., SFT, RLHF). This late intervention results in values being a "thin overlay" rather than deeply rooted, leading to several issues:
1.  **Fragile Alignment:** Aligned behaviors can be easily eroded by subsequent fine-tuning or reinforcement learning.
2.  **Jailbreak Vulnerability:** Frontier models remain susceptible to various jailbreak attacks.
3.  **Path Dependence:** Model training is path-dependent, meaning information introduced early has persistent effects. Pretraining offers broader context for robust generalization.
4.  **Suboptimal Design:** The assistant persona becomes a fragile interpolation of existing personas from the pretraining corpus, rather than a deeply ingrained, intentionally designed identity.

### Method

The authors propose **Synthetic Persona Pretraining (SPP)** to install a desired assistant persona from "token zero" (the beginning of pretraining). This involves a three-step process:

1.  **Data Annotation:** Approximately 10% of pretraining documents (including all harmful documents and an equal-token sample of benign ones) are annotated with value-aligned, first-person "reflections." These reflections are generated by a Qwen3.5-35B-A3B model, conditioned on a normative value constitution and explicit annotation guidelines, contextualizing the document content with respect to the constitution. A special `<assistant>` token precedes each reflection.
2.  **Pretraining with Reflections:** Models are pretrained using a standard cross-entropy loss on both the original documents and their synthetic reflections. This process embeds the desired persona among the multitude of other personas learned during pretraining. The study explores different injection points:
    *   **SPP{T0}:** Reflections appear throughout pretraining from the beginning.
    *   **SPP{MT}:** Reflections are introduced only during the midtraining (cooldown) stage at the end of pretraining.
    *   **SPP{T0,MT}:** Reflections are present throughout and re-introduced during midtraining.
    *   Baselines include **Vanilla** (no intervention) and **Filtered** (harmful documents removed).
    Models were trained up to 3B parameters on 500B tokens.
3.  **Persona Binding (Post-training):** All base models undergo supervised fine-tuning (SFT) on a specially generated synthetic-persona post-training (SP-SFT) dataset. This dataset uses the same constitution-conditioned generator to create assistant responses expressing the desired persona through first-person reflections, thereby "binding" the pre-trained persona to the assistant identity.

### Impact

SPP, especially when introduced from token zero, significantly improves alignment:

1.  **Enhanced Value Alignment:**
    *   **Constitution Following:** SPP models, particularly SPP{T0} and SPP{T0,MT}, demonstrate superior adherence to the constitution, especially on challenging cases. Midtraining-only intervention (SPP{MT}) shows minimal effect here.
    *   **Generalization to Moral Dilemmas:** Token zero models generalize beyond explicit constitutional rules, internalizing underlying principles. They prioritize values differently and make more aligned choices in out-of-distribution (OOD) moral dilemmas, reducing the misalignment rate. SPP{MT} does not yield this improvement.
2.  **Increased Jailbreak Robustness:** All SPP variants significantly outperform baselines in jailbreak robustness across eight diverse benchmarks. Interestingly, introducing reflections only during midtraining (SPP{MT}) is sufficient and sometimes even surpasses token zero interventions for jailbreak robustness.
3.  **Preservation of Capabilities:** The improvements in alignment and safety are achieved while generally preserving general capabilities and avoiding excessive refusal.
4.  **Scalability of Benefits:** The advantages of token zero interventions over midtraining increase with larger pretraining budgets (e.g., from 1.7B/100B tokens to 3B/500B tokens), particularly for harder and OOD evaluations.
5.  **Importance of Persona Binding:** The alignment gains from SPP are contingent on successful "persona binding" during post-training; if the post-training data does not reinforce the pre-trained persona, the benefits diminish.

In summary, the research establishes that shaping an LLM's values early in pretraining, from "token zero," is critical for deep, robust, and generalizable alignment, offering a more fundamental approach than post-training interventions alone.
